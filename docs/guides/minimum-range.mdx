`minRange` sets a minimum auto-fitted Y span in **data units**, shared by
`LiveChart` and `LiveChartSeries`. For a market moving just $0.30, whole-dollar
labels can all round to the same price. A floor of four display steps gives
those labels room:

```tsx
<LiveChart
  data={data}
  value={value}
  minRange={priceIncrement * 4}
  formatValue={formatPrice}
/>
```

The normal data fit and its margin run first. If the visible span after bounds
would be smaller than `minRange`, the fit widens around its midpoint, then
shifts off `nonNegative`'s zero floor or `maxValue`'s ceiling. A fit that is
already wide enough stays unchanged. Omit the prop for the existing auto-fit.

Use a positive, finite number. Zero, negative, and non-finite values are ignored;
a requested floor that would overflow the numeric bounds is ignored too. When
both hard bounds leave less room than the floor, the available span wins. For
example, `nonNegative maxValue={1} minRange={4}` fits within `0…1`.

Manual [`yRangeScale`](/guides/y-range-scale) applies **after** the fitted floor:
`minRange={4}` at `0.5×` gives a two-unit span. The floor controls auto-fit and
does not restrict deliberate manual zooming. Ordinary range easing still
applies; a static single-series chart re-settles when the floor changes.
Changing its manual zoom or zero-bound flag also re-settles without replacing data.

In the demo:

- Compare **Auto-fit** and **4 units** on **Near-flat** data. The line is the
  same; the whole-dollar labels stop repeating. The **Y span** readout reports
  the real bounds from the chart's UI-thread scale.
- Choose **Wide**: a four-unit floor leaves the wider data fit unchanged.
- Choose **Zero** or **Ceiling**, then **0.3 units**: the span remains `0.300`,
  even though the ordinary fit's margin crosses the hard bound.
- Choose **Both**, then **4 units**: the hard `0…1` domain wins.
- Switch between **Line**, **Candles**, and **Series** to compare both engines.
- In **Options**, enable **Static chart** for line/candle mode. Change the
  minimum in **Compare** to verify re-settling without replacing data. Also
  compare **Zoom 0.5×**, **1×**, and **2×**.

The history and clock are fixed for repeatable comparisons. Choose the floor
for your data precision; it does not change tick formatting or enforce a
specific number of grid labels. See [Axes & grid](/guides/axes-and-grid) for
tick spacing and formatting.
